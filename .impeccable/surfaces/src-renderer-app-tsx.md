## Scope and mode

- Scope: the complete SpeakSpace renderer, with Studio as the primary surface and all other routes inheriting one system.
- Mode: Operate.

## Audience, job, and task

- Students, researchers, creators, and knowledge workers capturing spoken material.
- Primary job: record or import speech, inspect a local transcript, ask the local model, and preserve useful knowledge.
- Primary action: begin a recording or upload audio from Studio.
- Follow-on work: organize results in Workspace and use Agent for bounded read-only investigation.

## Content and constraints

- Preserve all existing routes, behaviors, IPC flows, and in-progress working-tree changes.
- Keep SpeakSpace and the blue SS mark.
- Maintain Simplified Chinese and English, light/dark themes, font-size controls, keyboard access, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Do not invent customer claims, benchmarks, pricing, or cloud capabilities.

## Chosen direction

- Direction: Corpus Index Desk / 语料索引台.
- Decision reference: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/assigned.png` (critique reference, not an approved pixel specification).
- Execution: code-led across the full product.
- Materials: frost-white index fields, carbon-navy equipment rail, cobalt annotation rules, teal verified-local states, amber cautions.
- Component grammar: continuous ruled work surfaces; compact 8–16px corners; hairline dividers; border or soft offset shadow, never both.
- Typography: compact humanist system UI face for operational copy; tabular numerals for timestamps and measurements; no display-hero typography.
- Motion: one restrained pointer spotlight on local runtime status, fast state transitions elsewhere, and complete reduced-motion fallback.

## Memorable moment

Studio reads as one uninterrupted path from source material through transcript and local reasoning to preserved knowledge, with recording controls anchored to the desk rather than floating above it.

## Unresolved decisions

- None required for this redesign. Real user data will determine the final density of populated transcript and workspace states.
