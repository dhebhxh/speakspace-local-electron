# SpeakSpace Content

SpeakSpace organizes locally stored material for recording, reviewing, and AI-assisted conversation. This glossary defines the user-visible content and recovery language shared across those experiences.

## Language

**Workspace**:
A named collection that owns a set of Notes. When a Workspace enters the Trash, all of its Notes travel with it as one recoverable unit, including Notes that were already Trash Items.
_Avoid_: Folder, project

**Note**:
A reusable content item centered on a transcript and its related material. A Note belongs to one Workspace and can enter the Trash independently.
_Avoid_: Document, recording

**Note Attachment**:
Material whose lifecycle follows a Note, such as summaries, knowledge outputs, to-do items, and its managed recording. It is restored or permanently deleted with the Note rather than appearing as a separate Trash Item.
_Avoid_: Trash item, standalone item

**Conversation**:
An AI dialogue and its messages, owned independently from any Notes used as sources. A Conversation remains user content when a source Note enters the Trash or is permanently deleted.
_Avoid_: Note Attachment, generated output

**Trash**:
The recoverable holding area for removed Notes and Workspaces. Items in the Trash are absent from normal work views and remain recoverable indefinitely until the user explicitly permanently deletes them.
_Avoid_: Archive, recently deleted

**Trash Item**:
A Note or Workspace currently held in the Trash. A trashed Workspace is one Trash Item even though it contains Notes; its Notes do not also appear as separate Trash Items.
_Avoid_: Note Attachment

**Move to Trash**:
Remove a Note or Workspace from active work while preserving it as a Trash Item. Moving a Note takes effect immediately and offers a brief opportunity to undo the move; moving an entire Workspace first requires confirmation and then offers the same brief opportunity.
_Avoid_: Permanently delete, erase

**Restore**:
Return the same Trash Item and its retained state and relationships to active work. Restoration does not recreate the item or change its content timestamps.
_Avoid_: Recreate, import

**Permanently Delete**:
Irreversibly remove a Trash Item and the content whose lifecycle follows it, after explicit user confirmation.
_Avoid_: Delete, remove
